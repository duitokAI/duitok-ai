# Pokaya AI Frontend Conversion PRD

Last updated: 2026-05-29

## 1. Core Decision

Pokaya AI should not position itself as a TikTok Affiliate-only product.

Pokaya AI should position itself as:

> **An AI workflow hub for people who want to create content, promote products, and build online income opportunities with AI.**

TikTok Affiliate remains the first optimized workflow pack, but it is not the whole brand identity.

## 2. Target User

### Primary User

People who want to use AI to make money, but do not know how to start.

They are thinking:

- AI can make money, but I do not know what to do first.
- There are too many AI tools.
- I do not know how to prompt.
- I want to create content, promote products, sell something, or try affiliate.
- I want a ready path, not theory.
- RM79.80/month is acceptable if it feels like a full practical toolkit.

### Secondary User

TikTok Affiliate beginner, online seller, creator, small business operator.

They are thinking:

- I need more content.
- I need product images, videos, captions, hooks, prompts.
- I want to move faster than doing everything manually.

## 3. Product Promise

### Main Promise

> Pokaya AI helps beginners turn AI into content, product promotion, and online income workflows.

### Malay Public Promise

> Pokaya AI bantu anda guna AI untuk buat content, promote produk dan bina peluang income online dengan workflow yang siap guna.

### Avoid These Claims

Do not say:

- Guarantee income
- Confirm sales
- Confirm viral
- Passive income
- No work required

Use safer words:

- peluang income
- mula guna AI secara praktikal
- test lebih banyak idea
- buat content lebih cepat
- promote produk dengan workflow siap guna

## 4. Conversion Funnel

The homepage should follow this exact funnel:

1. **Urgency**: Why act now?
2. **Hero**: Why this matters to me?
3. **Trust proof**: Can I trust this enough to continue?
4. **Value anchors**: Why is it worth RM79.80?
5. **Pain**: Describe my stuck state.
6. **Problem cards**: Make my problems concrete.
7. **Workflow solution**: Show what Pokaya gives me.
8. **Real outputs**: Prove the AI output is real.
9. **Old way vs Pokaya way**: Make the difference obvious.
10. **TikTok Affiliate pack**: Show the first money workflow.
11. **Pricing**: Make the offer easy to buy.
12. **Checkout / CTA**: Reduce friction.
13. **FAQ**: Remove last objections.

### 5.14 FAQ Section

Purpose:

Remove skepticism and objections.

Title:

> Soalan biasa

FAQ:

**Adakah Pokaya AI guarantee saya dapat income?**  
Tidak. Pokaya AI tidak menjanjikan income. Pokaya AI membantu anda hasilkan content, idea, visual, video dan workflow dengan lebih cepat supaya anda boleh test lebih banyak peluang.

**Saya beginner, boleh guna?**  
Boleh. Pokaya AI dibina untuk pengguna yang mahu mula guna AI tanpa perlu belajar banyak tool atau prompt yang rumit.

**Adakah Pokaya AI hanya untuk TikTok Affiliate?**  
Tidak. Pokaya AI ialah platform workflow AI untuk content, sales dan online income. TikTok Affiliate ialah workflow pertama yang kami optimalkan secara mendalam.

**Apa beza Pokaya AI dengan ChatGPT?**  
ChatGPT ialah chatbot umum. Pokaya AI membungkus prompt, image, video dan content workflow menjadi tools yang lebih terus guna untuk content dan promotion.

**Apa yang saya dapat dalam plan Pro?**  
Anda dapat akses kepada Image AI, Video AI, Prompt Library, Auto Content, Storytelling, Clone Video, TikTok Affiliate workflow pack dan VIP group.

**Perlu top up credit lagi?**  
Jika generation menggunakan credit, anda hanya top up bila perlu. Plan Pro unlock platform, workflow dan rate generate. Kos generation perlu dipaparkan sebelum anda generate.

**Boleh cancel bila-bila?**  
Boleh. Anda boleh cancel bila-bila melalui akaun anda atau hubungi support.

**Boleh bayar guna FPX?**  
Boleh. Pokaya AI menyokong FPX online banking dan kaedah pembayaran Malaysia yang tersedia.

## 6. Visual Design Direction

### 6.1 Style

Use a premium but beginner-friendly conversion page.

Recommended:

- Dark hero with strong yellow/pink CTA
- Pokaya purple/coral brand accents
- Large readable Malay copy
- Real output cards
- Workflow board visuals
- Pricing card with strong contrast

Avoid:

- Looking too much like PeningLab orange/yellow identity
- Generic AI gradients only
- Abstract robot visuals without product output
- Tiny SaaS copy that feels corporate

### 6.2 Must-Have Visual Components

- Sticky promo bar
- Hero workflow board
- Problem cards
- Workflow cards
- Real output gallery
- Manual vs Pokaya comparison
- Pricing card
- FAQ accordion
