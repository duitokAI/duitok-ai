# Pokaya AI: AI Income Workflow Hub PRD

Last updated: 2026-05-29

## 1. Background

Pokaya AI is no longer positioned as a TikTok Affiliate-only tool.

The current audience being attracted is broader:

- People who want to use AI to make money
- Beginners who want to create content with AI
- Online sellers and affiliate beginners
- Creators who want ready-to-use prompts, image tools, video tools, and content workflows
- Users who do not want to learn many separate AI tools or prompt engineering

The product is structurally an **AI middle layer / AI app hub**. It packages AI models and workflows into direct-use tools. The first optimized vertical is TikTok Affiliate, but the front-end positioning should not lock the whole brand into TikTok Affiliate.

## 2. Product Positioning

### 2.1 One-Line Positioning

**Pokaya AI helps beginners use AI to create content, promote products, and build online income workflows without learning complicated tools.**

Malay-facing version:

**Pokaya AI ialah platform AI untuk bantu anda buat content, promote produk, dan bina peluang income online dengan workflow yang siap guna.**

### 2.2 Positioning Layers

| Layer | Meaning | Front-End Role |
|---|---|---|
| Brand | Pokaya AI | Broad AI app hub |
| Category | AI income workflow platform | What users feel they are buying |
| First vertical | TikTok Affiliate workflow | Specific use case and conversion hook |
| Tools | Image AI, Video AI, Prompt Library, Auto Content, Clone Video, Storytelling | Concrete paid value |

### 2.3 What Pokaya AI Is

- AI app hub
- AI workflow station
- Content and monetization tool suite
- Practical AI platform for creators, sellers, affiliates, and beginners

### 2.4 What Pokaya AI Is Not

- Not only a TikTok Affiliate tool
- Not only an image generator
- Not only a prompt library
- Not a course
- Not a guaranteed income scheme
- Not a generic chatbot wrapper

## 3. Target Users

### 3.1 Primary User

**AI income beginner**

Profile:

- Wants to use AI to make money
- May not have technical knowledge
- May not know which AI tool to use
- Wants fast content output
- Wants practical use cases, not theory
- Is willing to pay RM79.80/month if the value feels like a full tool suite

Core thought:

> I know AI can help me earn, but I do not know where to start or how to turn it into daily action.

### 3.2 Secondary Users

**TikTok Affiliate beginner**

- Wants product content ideas
- Wants video scripts and hooks
- Wants faster content production
- Wants affiliate workflow guidance

**Small online seller**

- Wants product images, promo videos, captions, and sales content
- Wants to save time and reduce content production cost

**Creator / content operator**

- Wants prompt packs, storytelling templates, clone-style content, and video/image tools

## 4. Core User Problem

Users do not primarily suffer from lack of AI tools. They suffer from:

- Too many tools
- Not knowing which tool to use
- Not knowing what to prompt
- Not knowing how AI output connects to money
- Not having repeatable content workflows
- Feeling that AI is powerful but messy

Pokaya AI should solve this by turning AI into packaged workflows.

## 5. Product Promise

### 5.1 Primary Promise

**Use AI to create more monetizable content faster.**

### 5.2 Supporting Promises

- Generate image content quickly
- Generate video content quickly
- Access ready-to-use prompts
- Create content ideas and scripts
- Use TikTok Affiliate workflows without starting from zero
- Join a VIP group for updates and new workflows

### 5.3 Claims To Avoid

Do not claim:

- Guaranteed income
- Guaranteed sales
- Guaranteed viral content
- Guaranteed TikTok success
- Passive income without work

Safer phrasing:

- "bina peluang income"
- "mula guna AI secara praktikal"
- "buat content lebih cepat"
- "promote produk dengan workflow siap guna"
- "test lebih banyak idea content"

## 6. Pricing Strategy

### 6.1 Current Price

- Monthly subscription: **RM79.80/month**
- Promo display can round visually to **RM79** or **RM75** only if checkout matches clearly.

Recommendation:

Use one consistent price in final UI:

**RM79.80 / bulan**

If a promo price is used, make the checkout price and front-end price match exactly.

### 6.2 Pricing Perception

RM79.80/month should feel like:

- A complete AI tool suite
- Cheaper than subscribing to multiple AI tools separately
- Practical for people trying to build side income
- Serious enough to imply commitment, but still beginner-accessible

### 6.3 Pricing Page Message

Current copy:

> Akses penuh untuk seller serius.

Recommended replacement:

> **Akses penuh untuk bina income dengan AI.**

Supporting line:

> Satu platform untuk image AI, video AI, prompt, content workflow dan tools khas untuk creator, seller & affiliate.

## 11. FAQ Copy

### Q1: Adakah Pokaya AI hanya untuk TikTok Affiliate?

Tidak. Pokaya AI ialah platform workflow AI untuk content, sales dan online income. TikTok Affiliate ialah workflow pertama yang kami optimalkan secara mendalam.

### Q2: Saya beginner, boleh guna?

Boleh. Pokaya AI dibina untuk pengguna yang mahu mula guna AI tanpa perlu belajar banyak tool atau prompt yang rumit.

### Q3: Adakah Pokaya AI guarantee saya dapat income?

Tidak. Pokaya AI tidak menjanjikan income. Pokaya AI membantu anda hasilkan content, idea, visual, video dan workflow dengan lebih cepat supaya anda boleh test lebih banyak peluang.

### Q4: Apa yang saya dapat dalam plan Pro?

Anda dapat akses kepada Image AI, Video AI, Prompt Library, Auto Content, Storytelling, Clone Video, TikTok Affiliate workflow pack dan VIP group.

### Q5: Boleh cancel bila-bila?

Boleh. Anda boleh cancel bila-bila melalui akaun anda.

### 12.2 Product Activation Metrics

- First generation completed

- First video generated
- First prompt used
- First workflow completed

### 12.3 Positioning Validation

User interviews and support messages should indicate that users understand Pokaya AI as:

- AI tools for making content
- AI platform for online income workflows
- Practical AI tools for creators/sellers/affiliates

If users still describe it only as "TikTok Affiliate tool", the homepage positioning is still too narrow.

## 13. Acceptance Criteria

- The first screen communicates broad AI income/content workflow value.
- TikTok Affiliate appears as a use case, not the brand definition.
- Pricing copy makes RM79.80/month feel like a complete AI workflow suite.
- Feature bullets are outcome-oriented, not only "Access..." labels.
- No UI copy guarantees income or virality.
- Malay copy is clear, direct, and beginner-friendly.
- The product can later add non-TikTok workflows without changing the brand positioning again.
