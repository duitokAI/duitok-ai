# PeningLab Reference Audit For Pokaya AI

Last updated: 2026-05-29

Source reviewed:

- `https://peninglab.com/`
- `https://peninglab.com/affiliate`

Important note:

This document is for structural and strategic reference. Do not copy PeningLab's exact wording, testimonial text, customer names, generated visuals, UI assets, or claims. Pokaya AI should borrow the sales architecture and adapt it to the broader **AI income workflow hub** positioning.

## 1. What PeningLab Does Well

PeningLab sells with a complete single-page conversion funnel:

1. Urgency bar at the top
2. Hero with a painful contrast
3. Fast promise and clear CTA
4. Trust micro-proof under CTA
5. Three core value anchors: speed, price, simplicity
6. Emotional "pain reality" section
7. Problem cards
8. Feature cards framed as "weapons"
9. Real output/demo section
10. Old way vs new way comparison
11. Testimonials
12. Pricing card with countdown and discount framing
13. Payment form on the same page
14. FAQ
15. Affiliate program page for people who want to earn by promoting

This flow is stronger than a normal SaaS feature page because it repeatedly answers:

- Why now?
- Why this problem?
- Why this tool?
- Why this price?
- Why should I trust it?
- What do I do next?

## 2. What Pokaya AI Should Copy Structurally

### 2.1 Top Urgency Bar

PeningLab uses a sticky urgency bar with promo countdown and remaining slots.

Pokaya should use the same component type, but with safer copy:

Recommended Pokaya version:

> Promo pengguna awal tamat dalam {time}. Tinggal {slots} slot harga RM79.80/bulan.

Avoid:

- Fake scarcity
- Exact "seller dah claim hari ni" wording unless backed by real data
- Overly aggressive countdown if it never expires

### 2.2 Hero Contrast

PeningLab's hero is built around:

> Competitor is already posting. You are still thinking.

Pokaya's broader version should not be TikTok-only.

Recommended Pokaya version:

> Orang lain dah guna AI untuk buat content. Anda masih cari tool yang betul.

Alternative:

> Semua orang cakap AI boleh buat duit. Anda masih tak tahu nak mula dari mana.

Hero support copy:

> Pokaya AI kumpulkan image AI, video AI, prompt library dan workflow siap guna supaya anda boleh hasilkan content, promote produk dan test peluang income online dengan lebih cepat.

### 2.3 Trust Row Under CTA

PeningLab uses small proof points like rating, active users, and money-back.

Pokaya should use:

- 30-day money back
- Cancel bila-bila
- FPX online banking
- Image AI + Video AI included

Avoid inflated user count until real.

### 2.4 Three Value Anchors

PeningLab's anchors:

- Speed
- Cheap price
- Easy

Pokaya should use:

- CEPAT: buat content lebih laju
- SENANG: workflow siap guna, bukan prompt kosong
- LENGKAP: image, video, prompt, content, affiliate pack

Suggested copy:

| Anchor | Title | Body |
|---|---|---|
| CEPAT | Content siap lebih cepat | Generate idea, visual, script dan video tanpa buka 10 tool berbeza. |
| SENANG | Tak perlu pandai prompt | Pilih workflow, isi maklumat ringkas, Pokaya bantu susun output. |
| LENGKAP | Satu hub AI untuk mula | Image AI, Video AI, Prompt Library, Auto Content, Storytelling dan workflow affiliate dalam satu platform. |

### 2.5 Pain Reality Section

PeningLab uses a strong "realiti pahit" section.

Pokaya should keep this because the target user is confused and overwhelmed.

Recommended title:

> Ramai nak guna AI untuk buat duit. Tapi kebanyakan orang stuck di langkah pertama.

Support:

> Terlalu banyak tool, terlalu banyak prompt, terlalu banyak tutorial. Akhirnya content tak siap, produk tak dipromote, idea hanya tinggal idea.

### 2.6 Problem Cards

PeningLab has five problem cards. Pokaya should adapt them:

1. Tak tahu nak mula
2. Terlalu banyak AI tool
3. Tak pandai prompt
4. Content lambat siap
5. Susah konsisten

These fit Pokaya's wider AI income audience better than seller-only pain cards.

### 2.7 Feature Cards As "Weapons"

PeningLab frames features as "5 weapons".

Pokaya can use the same framing but change the content:

Section title:

> 5 workflow AI untuk mula hasilkan content yang boleh dipromote.

Cards:

- Image AI: gambar produk, creator shot, visual content
- Video AI: UGC, reels, short ads
- Prompt Library: sales, hook, caption, storytelling
- Auto Content: idea to script to visual/video plan
- TikTok Affiliate Pack: first optimized income workflow

Avoid saying Pokaya can guarantee income.

### 2.8 Real Output Section

PeningLab shows videos and labels them as real output.

Pokaya should absolutely copy this section type.

Recommended title:

> Output sebenar dari Pokaya AI.

Body:

> Contoh image, video, prompt dan content angle yang boleh dijana dalam workflow Pokaya.

Needed assets:

- 5-8 image outputs
- 3-5 short video outputs
- 3 prompt cards
- 1 TikTok Affiliate workflow example

This is a high-priority section because AI tools need visual proof.

### 2.9 Old Way vs Pokaya Way

PeningLab's "Manual vs PeningLab" section is one of the best parts.

Pokaya should copy the comparison structure:

Left: Cara lama

- Buka banyak AI tools
- Cari prompt di internet
- Cuba satu-satu sampai pening
- Generate image di satu tempat
- Generate video di tempat lain
- Tulis caption manual
- Susun content plan sendiri

Right: Cara Pokaya AI

- Pilih workflow
- Isi produk / idea / niche
- Generate prompt, image, script atau video
- Simpan output dalam satu tempat
- Guna TikTok Affiliate pack jika mahu mula promote produk
- Ulang dan test lebih banyak angle

This section makes the "AI middle layer" positioning easy to understand.

### 2.10 Pricing Card

PeningLab's pricing card is strong because it uses:

- Clear old price vs promo price
- Big price
- Feature checklist
- Trust row
- CTA
- Payment explanation below

Pokaya should copy the layout logic.

Recommended Pokaya pricing title:

> Akses penuh untuk bina income dengan AI.

Feature bullets:

- Image AI untuk gambar produk & content
- Video AI untuk TikTok, Reels & ads
- Prompt Library untuk sales & marketing
- Auto Content idea, hook & script
- Clone Video & Storytelling tools
- TikTok Affiliate workflow pack
- VIP group & workflow updates

Price:

> RM79.80 / bulan

CTA:

> Mula guna Pokaya AI sekarang

### 2.11 Same-Page Checkout

PeningLab places registration/payment directly after pricing.

Pokaya should consider this if checkout friction is high:

- Name
- WhatsApp
- Email
- Agree terms
- Pay with FPX / DuitNow QR

This is especially useful for Malaysia users who expect WhatsApp support.

### 2.12 FAQ

PeningLab's FAQ handles operational doubts.

Pokaya should include:

- Adakah Pokaya AI guarantee income?
- Saya beginner, boleh guna?
- Adakah ini hanya untuk TikTok Affiliate?
- Apa beza Pokaya dengan ChatGPT?
- Apa beza membership dengan credit?
- Boleh cancel bila-bila?
- Boleh bayar guna FPX?

## 3. What Pokaya Should Not Copy

Do not copy these directly:

- "Kompetitor dah post 10 video. Anda baru fikir."
- The exact testimonial text and names
- "Termurah di planet ni"
- "Video orang dah viral? Curi formula dia."
- Specific customer counts unless Pokaya has real data
- Fake live purchase notifications
- Exact dark/orange/yellow visual identity
- Exact pricing numbers or discount claims if they are not true

Use toned-down, trust-preserving versions.

## 4. Affiliate Page Lessons

PeningLab's `/affiliate` page is very relevant to Pokaya's "users want AI income" traffic.

It sells a second path:

- Not just use the AI tool
- Earn by promoting the AI tool

Pokaya should create a future page:

> Earn with Pokaya

Position:

> Promote Pokaya AI and earn recurring commission when users subscribe.

Suggested structure:

1. Hero: Earn recurring commission by promoting Pokaya AI
2. Commission calculator
3. What you promote: Image AI, Video AI, Prompt Library, Auto Content, Affiliate Pack
4. How it works: Apply, get link, share, earn, cash out
5. Benefits: recurring commission, Malaysia payout, free trial/credits for promoters
6. Apply form

This should be P1 or P2, after core pricing and homepage positioning are fixed.
