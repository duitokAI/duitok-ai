# Pokaya AI Brand Identity PRD

Last updated: 2026-05-29

## 1. Background

Pokaya AI is rebranding to **Pokaya AI** after TikTok developer review rejected app names containing `Tik` or `Tok`. The new brand has already been decided:

- Product name: **Pokaya AI**
- Primary domain: **pokaya.ai**
- Market: Malaysia sellers, TikTok Shop sellers, affiliate sellers, side-income beginners
- Mascot concept: a wallet character that represents earning more money through higher content output

The current visual direction is useful as a starting point:

- Purple wallet mascot
- Coral / pink AI accents
- Friendly 3D soft-plastic style
- Selling / discount / video / AI symbols

But the current assets still say **Pokaya AI** and must be redesigned into a clean Pokaya system instead of simple text replacement.

## 2. Objective

Create a full **Pokaya AI brand asset system** that can be used across:

- Homepage
- Login/register
- Studio sidebar
- Billing/checkout
- Agent page
- SOP modal/page
- Auto Post extension
- TikTok Developer app profile
- Cloudflare/Render/GitHub branding
- Social profile avatar

The output should make Pokaya AI feel like:

> A friendly money-making AI operator for Malaysia sellers.

It should not feel like:

- A TikTok clone
- A childish toy brand
- A generic AI SaaS logo
- A crypto wallet app
- A gambling/casino brand

## 4. Naming And Wordmark

### 4.1 Primary Wordmark

Text:

```text
Pokaya AI
```

Rules:

- `Pokaya` must be readable at small size.
- `AI` can use the coral/pink gradient accent.
- Avoid making the `AI` too close to TikTok/play-button branding.
- Avoid using `Tok`, `Tik`, or visual tricks that suggest the old name.
- The wordmark should work both with and without the mascot.

### 4.2 Typography Direction

Recommended:

- Rounded geometric sans
- Heavy but friendly weight
- Slightly premium SaaS feel
- Large counters and clean edges for readability

Avoid:

- Overly bubbly kids font
- Thin futuristic AI font
- Aggressive gaming font
- Handwritten style

## 5. Mascot Direction

### 5.1 Mascot Role

The mascot is **Pokaya's wallet operator**.

It should communicate:

- "I help you manage content opportunities."
- "I help you test more selling angles."
- "I help your platform move toward more money."

It should not look like:

- A literal bank mascot
- A casino/gambling symbol
- A crypto wallet icon
- A TikTok mascot
- A children's cartoon character

### 5.2 Character Design

Base:

- Purple wallet body
- Rounded square shape
- Soft 3D material
- Small arms and legs
- Friendly face
- One hand may hold a discount card or sales card
- Wallet may contain product/video/content cards

Expression:

- Confident wink or focused smile
- Helpful, energetic
- Not overly silly
- Not too baby-like

Operator Elements:

- Small AI badge
- Tiny dashboard card
- Growth chart card
- Content/video card
- Spark marks used sparingly

### 5.3 Wallet Symbolism

The wallet represents:

- More money
- Saved selling opportunities
- Generated content assets
- Practical business utility

Do not overuse:

- Cash flying everywhere
- Coins explosion
- Luxury/gold flex
- Casino-like money symbols

### 6.2 Secondary Colors

- Small amount of warm yellow/gold may be used for money/growth accents.
- Avoid making gold the dominant brand color.
- Avoid green dominance because it may push the brand toward finance/crypto.

### 6.3 Color Rules

- The primary visual should still read as purple + coral.
- Coral should signal action, AI, selling, generation, and growth.
- Purple should signal brand ownership and trust.
- The palette must work on both white and dark purple backgrounds.

## 11. Quality Bar

### 11.1 Must Pass

- Reads clearly as `Pokaya AI`.
- Mascot reads as wallet at first glance.
- App icon reads at 128px.
- Favicon reads at 32px.
- No `Pokaya` text remains in new assets.
- No `Tik` / `Tok` in product name.
- No TikTok logo, TikTok color split, or official-looking TikTok mark.
- Works on white and dark UI.
- Looks premium enough for a paid RM69/month product.

### 11.2 Reject If

- Text is misspelled.
- Wordmark is distorted.
- Mascot looks too childish or baby-like.
- Wallet looks like a generic crypto wallet app.
- Design looks like gambling/casino.
- Too many floating symbols make it noisy.
- AI/play icons dominate over the wallet and wordmark.
- Logo only works as a large image and fails in sidebar/favicon.

## 13. Recommended Direction

Choose **Premium Wallet Operator**.

Reason:

- It keeps the strongest part of the current assets: the wallet mascot.
- It supports the money/growth meaning of Pokaya.
- It can still be cute without becoming childish.
- It works better for a paid product than a pure cartoon direction.
- It gives the brand a memorable asset for GEO, social, onboarding, and Agent.
