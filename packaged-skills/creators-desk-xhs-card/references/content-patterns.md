# Content Patterns

Use this when turning an article, tweet, script, or notes into Creators Desk cards.

## One-Card TIP Cover

Use this structure when the user asks for a single cover or a `TIP 01 / TIP 02` card.

```text
TIP: TIP 01 / TIP 02 / ...
Topic chip: GPT Image 2 产品图教程
Headline: 用 AI 把【痛点/对象】做成【结果】
Subtitle: 不是【误区】，而是【可复用方法】
Main module label: 核心 3 件事 / 只改 5 处 / 本集只讲
Tokens: 5 short concepts
Step cards: 3 short steps
Applications: 6 use cases
Quote: 1 sentence takeaway
```

## Article Distillation

Extract only the visual argument:

- Pain point: what problem the reader has.
- Promise: what the method helps them produce.
- Core standard: 3-5 principles.
- Process: 3 concise steps.
- Applications: 6 concrete use cases.
- Takeaway: one memorable sentence.

Do not copy long passages from source text into the image. Put details, full prompts, and examples in post body if needed.

## Copy Guidelines

- Prefer user-facing wording: `小白照着改`, `直接拿去生成`, `先做这一步`.
- Replace technical labels with practical labels.
- Keep each card title under 14 Chinese characters when possible.
- Keep step body text under 24 Chinese characters when possible.
- Do not put full product prompt text inside the image.
