# Creators Desk Card Brand

Use this reference when adapting the bundled template or creating matching cards.

## Typography

- Primary font: `PingFang SC`.
- Fallbacks: `PingFang HK`, `PingFang TC`, `Hiragino Sans GB`, sans-serif.
- Use heavy weights for titles, but keep Chinese letter spacing modest. Avoid over-compressed Chinese.

## Visual Tone

- Xiaohongshu-friendly, bright, clean, tutorial-first.
- Use large hook titles and short, scannable modules.
- Keep the Creators Desk logo small in the top-left.
- Keep top-right pill for category/version, e.g. `小白填空版`.

## Palette

- Ink: `#18151d`
- Muted body: `#68606f`
- Hot coral: `#ff4f38`
- Rose: `#c12b62`
- Plum: `#32103a`
- Cyan: `#13b8c5`
- Background: warm white to pale pink gradient with a subtle grid.

## Composition Rules

- Size: 1080 x 1440.
- Top area: logo, category pill, TIP chip, topic chip.
- Hero: one direct headline, 2-4 lines max.
- Middle: one main insight module, then three short step cards.
- Lower: one application grid, then one quote/callout.
- Do not include full prompt text or long paragraphs in the image.
- Avoid product sample images unless the user explicitly asks for them.
